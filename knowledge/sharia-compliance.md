# Sharia-compliant employers

Some users only want to work for employers whose business is Sharia-compliant; others
prefer it; others don't mind. `/setup` **asks** every user and stores the answer in
`profile/search.md` as one of:

- **required** – non-compliant employers are a deal-breaker (gate in `knowledge/evaluation.md`)
- **preferred** – compliant employers get a small bonus; non-compliant ones are flagged, not removed
- **no preference** – don't check (and don't mention it)

Never assume the answer from someone's name, nationality or religion. Ask.

## How to classify an employer

Judge the **employer's core business**, not the job title (the same IT job can be
fine at one company and not at another). Check the company's own website or annual
report; don't guess from the name.

| Class | Typical examples | With "required" | With "preferred" |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Compliant** | Islamic banks and Islamic finance/takaful companies; most technology, telecom, oil and gas, construction, healthcare, education, retail and government employers | OK | +5 points |
| **Not compliant** | Conventional (interest-based) banks, lenders and conventional insurers; businesses built on alcohol, gambling, pork, or adult entertainment | Gate: skip | Flag clearly, no bonus |
| **Unclear** | Large groups with some non-compliant business lines, international hotel chains, music or entertainment companies, tobacco, companies whose revenue mix you can't verify | Ask the user before scoring | Flag as "unclear" |

Notes for Kuwait:

- Kuwaiti banks are either Islamic or conventional. Islamic banks include Kuwait Finance
  House (KFH), Boubyan Bank, Warba Bank and Kuwait International Bank (KIB). Conventional
  banks include NBK, Gulf Bank, Burgan Bank, Al Ahli Bank of Kuwait (ABK) and Commercial
  Bank of Kuwait (CBK) ([Global Finance, March 2024](https://gfmag.com/banking/top-10-banks-in-kuwait/)).
  Banks merge and convert, so confirm on the bank's own site ("Islamic banking",
  "Sharia Supervisory Board").
- An Islamic window or Islamic product inside a conventional bank does not make the bank
  itself compliant; treat it as **unclear** and ask.
- Alcohol is not sold in Kuwait, so a hotel operating in Kuwait is usually fine; the parent
  chain's business elsewhere is a question for the user (**unclear**).

## What to say to the user

- State the classification and the reason in one line, with the source you checked.
- For **unclear** cases, explain what is mixed and let the user decide. Do not issue
  religious rulings; if the user wants a ruling, suggest they ask a scholar they trust.
- Record the user's decision for that employer in `profile/search.md` under
  "Employer decisions" so they are not asked twice.
